Method

Diagnose. Design. Defend.

The same three-phase discipline runs underneath every division, whether the deliverable is a system, a contract, or a filing.

I

Diagnose

Audit the current structure — systems, contracts, controls, org design — and identify exactly where they conflict, duplicate, or fail to hold up under scrutiny.

II

Design

Architect the fix: ERP configuration, governance documents, process maps, and the operating model that closes the gaps the diagnosis found.

III

Defend

Carry the design through implementation, and if it's contested, through arbitration or dispute resolution — with the documentation to back it.
